Fast maker (paper, live)

experiment

Quotes both sides at the best price on 8 crypto markets (all but BTC), 10 contracts a side, and pulls the side in the path when the coin jumps on Coinbase. Paper only: fills are decided from real Kalshi trades with the same queue model as the simulation. Two identical copies run at different speeds: AWS Ohio (~15 ms, next to Kalshi) and New York (~30 ms, our droplet). The simulation's out-of-sample result was about +$9.50 per 15-minute period at 15 ms and +$6.75 at 30 ms. "Spread earned" values leftover contracts at the mid when quoting stops.

stopped

Stopped on 2026-09-29 after 255 trades: won 76.1% while paying 76.3¢ on average, so no edge (−$33.82 on $100). It briefly looked positive, then faded to zero, matching the 11-day historical replay. Results below are final.

Crypto only (BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL, DOGE, HYPE, ZEC, BNB, NEAR). If the favorite is priced 60–85¢ with 50, 40, 30 or 20 seconds left, buy it at the next quote. Hypothesis: crypto settles on a 60-second average, so late favorites are underpriced. Forward-tested since 2026-09-26 with no backfill. It only works if the win rate beats the average price paid, so edge below is win rate minus price. About 1,100 trades are needed to tell a 2.5¢ edge from luck.
